ABOUT THE ARTIST

BEAUTY,

Ishu Tiwari is a Delhi-based makeup artist trained at the prestigious UK International London Beauty School. Her approach views makeup as a refined form of self-expression—moving seamlessly between soft everyday radiance, glamorous occasion makeup, luxury bridal aesthetics, and bold editorial concepts.

Whether curating a timeless bridal glow or crafting statement eye artistry, Ishu focuses on skin prep, color harmony, and enhancing every client's natural features.

THE ISHU EXPERIENCE

CONSULTATION
